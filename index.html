When reporting a problem

These details help us find the same place or repeat the same steps.

  • Your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the app version shown under ⋯ on the world map.
  • The destination or region, the steps you took, and any message you saw.
  • If a screenshot would help, crop out faces and private details. You can report a problem without sending an original photo or a collection backup.

Common questions

How do I add a photo without a location?

Tap “Add a photo” on the world map and select an image. Choose its region, adjust the crop inside the outline, then tap “Place on map”. If the photo has GPS coordinates, you can accept or change the suggested region.

How do I change the photo shown on the map?

Open the region’s album and tap “Set as map cover” on the photo you want. You can adjust the crop when viewing the photo. The world, country and challenge maps all use that cover.

How do I move my collection to a new iPhone or iPad?

On the old device, open ⋯ → Collection backup and restore → Save backup file. Transfer the .tabiseiha file to the new device, select “Restore from backup”, and review the summary. Confirming replaces the collection currently on that device. Save a separate backup first if it already contains photos you want to keep.

How do I report a place name or boundary?

Email the country or territory, the region name as shown in the app, and what you expected to see. A link to a local mapping or statistics authority is helpful. The built-in atlas works offline and can reflect an earlier administrative boundary.
