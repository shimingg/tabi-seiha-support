Privacy policy

SHIHMING CHIU develops Tabi Seiha. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app, this support website, and information you choose to send by email.

Effective and last updated · November 26, 2026

Your photos and location

The app saves a copy of each image you choose, a preview, its region and cover crop on your device. It uses this information to draw your map and keeps your progress, stamps, first-unlock dates, next stop and language setting there as well.

If the selected image contains GPS coordinates, the app reads them on your device to suggest a region. You can choose a region manually. The app does not request your current location or send your collection to the developer. It requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Sharing a map or milestone

Sharing creates a PNG of the map or progress card you preview. Other people may see the photos, places, dates or progress visible on it. The image does not include your original image files or your full collection backup. You choose the destination in the system share sheet. Any service you select handles the shared image under its own privacy policy.

Keeping, deleting and backing up a collection

Photos remain in the app until you remove them, replace the collection or delete the app. Removing a photo from a region album removes the app’s copy. The source image in Photos or Files stays where you saved it. A region’s current progress updates when its last photo is removed. Stamps already earned and first-unlock history are retained.

A .tabiseiha backup contains all imported originals, including any embedded metadata such as GPS, plus previews, album assignments, cover crops, stamps, unlock dates and the next stop. The app does not add a password to this file. Choose a private storage location. After you review and confirm a restore, the saved collection replaces the current one.

To remove the whole local collection, choose Delete App in your device settings. Offload App keeps its documents and data. Exported files and shared images must be managed separately wherever you saved or sent them. Depending on your settings, Apple device backups may also contain app data. The app has no automatic cross-device sync and the developer cannot recover or erase your local collection remotely.

When you email support

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, display name, message and any attachments you send. These are used to reply, investigate and follow up. Email is handled through Gmail. Messages are retained while needed for those purposes and reviewed for deletion when they are no longer needed.

You can use the address below to ask about, correct or request deletion of an earlier support message. Identify the message by its subject or date. Please leave out any personal photos, exact coordinates or backup files that are unnecessary for your request.

This website and other services

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. The developer has added no analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or contact forms here. A page’s URL may indicate the language you selected.

Opening a support or source link connects to its host, which may receive ordinary web request information. The app does not attach your photos, coordinates or collection to those links. The following providers explain their own practices.

  • Apple device backups
  • GitHub Pages and IP addresses
  • Google Privacy Policy

Changes to this policy

Updates to the app’s handling of information will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. For a question about this policy, contact the developer below.
